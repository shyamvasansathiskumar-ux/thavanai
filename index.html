The law

India's privacy law now expects that data to be deleted.

  1. 2023DPDP Act passedSection 8(7): erase personal data once its purpose is served.
  2. 13 Nov 2025Rules notifiedMost duties begin 18 months later.
  3. 13 May 2027Erasure duty startsColleges, and their clubs, must be ready.

Thavanai helps clubs build the habit now. It is a record-keeping aid, not a compliance certificate.

The life of one collection

Thavanai gives every collection a due date.

25 SepRecord

132 team registrations for Hack the Campus are logged — the event, the purpose, the source, which kinds of data and where they are kept.

RuleDue date

30 days after the event: delete by 9 November. Nobody types the date — a retention policy works it out.

2 NovRemind

The status turns “Due in 7 days” in amber, then “Overdue” in red, and the collection joins the overdue report.

DeleteProve

The club records how it was deleted. The log and the status save in one transaction, and the record locks.

The one design decision

Thavanai never stores anyone's personal data.

It stores the label, not what is in the box: what kind of data, how many people, where it is kept. Text that looks like a phone number, email, register number or ID number is refused.

What the web version keeps — and adds

  • Same hashing. PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256, 600,000 iterations, 16-byte salt, constant-time compare — through the browser's Web Crypto.
  • Same database rules. Seven tables, five CHECKs, foreign keys and four triggers, enforced by an SQLite engine in the page.
  • Bound values only. Every query is a prepared statement. ' OR '1'='1 is just a wrong username.
  • Nothing leaves the tab. No server, no analytics, no cookies. A strict Content Security Policy allows no outside scripts.
  • Stricter guard. Phone numbers in groups, full 13-digit register numbers and PAN-style IDs are refused too.
  • Checks on every write. Club changes are refused for non-admins in the data layer, not only hidden on screen.
